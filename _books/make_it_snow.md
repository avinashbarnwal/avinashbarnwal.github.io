*By Denise Persson*

Snowflake's CMO Denise Persson and CRO Chris Degnan share how they built the go-to-market engine that took the company from startup to multibillion-dollar revenue.

## Key ideas

- **Sales and marketing as one team.** Shared goals, shared data and tight alignment drive growth.
- **Hire for ownership.** Bring in people who act like owners and raise the bar.
- **Focus on the customer.** Value-based selling and consumption-based pricing tie success to customer outcomes.
- **Build repeatable playbooks.** Scale by turning what works into processes, then keep improving them.
- **Culture scales growth.** Values and accountability keep the organisation moving fast as it grows.

## Takeaway

Hypergrowth comes from tightly aligned teams, customer focus and disciplined, repeatable execution.
