*By Morgan Housel*

A follow-up to The Psychology of Money, Morgan Housel looks at the other side of wealth: how to spend in ways that actually make life better.

## Key ideas

- **Spending reveals values.** How you use money says more about you than how much you have.
- **Independence is the best purchase.** Money that buys control over your time beats money that buys status.
- **Beware of comparison.** Spending to impress others is a game with no finish line.
- **Enough is a skill.** Knowing when you have enough protects you from endless wanting.
- **Personal, not universal.** There is no single right way to spend; align it with what you truly value.

## Takeaway

Spend deliberately on freedom, relationships and experiences you care about, not on signals for others.
