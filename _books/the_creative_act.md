*By Rick Rubin*

Legendary music producer Rick Rubin offers short reflections on creativity as a way of living, not a special talent reserved for artists.

## Key ideas

- **Everyone is a creator.** Simply by living and noticing, we are all making something.
- **Awareness is the source.** Ideas come from paying close attention to the world and to yourself.
- **Stay open and curious.** Approach work with a beginner's mind and let go of fixed expectations.
- **Play before judgement.** Separate the generating phase from the editing phase; criticism too early kills ideas.
- **Make the work for yourself.** Chasing an audience dilutes the work; serve the work first.
- **Finish and release.** Perfectionism blocks growth; complete a piece and move on to the next.

## Takeaway

Creativity is a practice of attention and openness. Show up, notice more, and let the work lead.
