*By Richard P. Feynman*

A collection of funny, candid stories from the life of Nobel-winning physicist Richard Feynman, from fixing radios as a boy to Los Alamos, Brazil and Caltech.

## Key ideas

- **Curiosity as a way of life.** Feynman learns drumming, drawing, safecracking and languages just because they interest him.
- **Think from first principles.** He distrusts jargon and memorisation and insists on understanding things for himself.
- **Don't fool yourself.** His 'cargo cult science' talk warns about the easy self-deception in research.
- **Playfulness fuels discovery.** Watching a spinning plate in a cafeteria led to ideas that fed into his Nobel work.
- **Ignore status.** He treats generals and students alike and is unimpressed by titles.

## Takeaway

Stay curious, have fun, and be honest with yourself; real understanding comes from working things out on your own.
