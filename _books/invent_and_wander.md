*By Jeff Bezos*

A collection of Jeff Bezos's Amazon shareholder letters, speeches and interviews, with an introduction by Walter Isaacson.

## Key ideas

- **It's always Day 1.** Fight stagnation by staying customer-obsessed, resisting proxies and deciding quickly.
- **Customer obsession.** Start with the customer and work backwards.
- **Long-term thinking.** Accept short-term pain for long-term value.
- **Two types of decisions.** Irreversible 'one-way doors' deserve care; reversible 'two-way doors' should be made fast.
- **Disagree and commit.** Teams can move forward even without full agreement.
- **Regret minimisation.** Choose the path you will not regret at 80.

## Takeaway

Invention requires wandering: experiment, accept failure and keep the customer at the centre.
