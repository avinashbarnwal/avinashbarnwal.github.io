*By David A. Sinclair*

Harvard geneticist David Sinclair argues that ageing is a process that can be slowed, and perhaps one day reversed, rather than an inevitable fate.

## Key ideas

- **Information theory of ageing.** Ageing results from a loss of epigenetic information, like scratches on a CD.
- **Longevity genes.** Sirtuins and related pathways respond to stress and help repair cells.
- **Hormesis.** Mild stressors like fasting, exercise and cold exposure can switch on protective pathways.
- **Reprogramming.** Research on resetting the epigenome hints that cells could be made 'younger'.
- **Society must prepare.** Longer lives raise big questions for healthcare, work and the planet.

## Takeaway

Ageing may be treatable. Lifestyle choices matter, and science may soon extend healthy years.
