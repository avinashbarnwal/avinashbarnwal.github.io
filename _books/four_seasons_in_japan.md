*By Nick Bradley*

A gentle novel about a translator in Tokyo who finds a forgotten book on a train and becomes absorbed in translating it, a story within a story set across a year of seasons.

## Key ideas

- **Stories that find you.** A chance discovery reshapes the translator's sense of purpose.
- **Translation as connection.** Moving words between languages becomes a way of understanding other people and herself.
- **Seasons and change.** The rhythm of the Japanese seasons mirrors growth, loss and renewal in the characters' lives.
- **Intergenerational bonds.** The book-within-the-book explores the bond between a young person and an older guardian.

## Takeaway

A calm, warm read about finding direction, the power of books and the small kindnesses that link strangers.
