*By Elisa Farri*

A practical guide for managers on how to use generative AI in everyday work, written by Elisa Farri and Gabriele Rosani.

## Key ideas

- **AI as a thinking partner.** Beyond automating tasks, GenAI can help managers think, challenge assumptions and explore options.
- **Ask better questions.** Good prompts give context, roles and constraints, and are refined through conversation.
- **Keep human judgement.** Managers stay accountable; AI output must be checked for errors and bias.
- **Start with real use cases.** Apply AI to concrete tasks like analysis, writing, brainstorming and decision support.
- **Build team capability.** Encourage experimentation and share what works across the team.

## Takeaway

Treat GenAI as a collaborator that sharpens your thinking, while keeping responsibility for the final call.
