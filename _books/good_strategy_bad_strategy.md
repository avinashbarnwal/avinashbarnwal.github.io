*By Richard P. Rumelt*

Richard Rumelt explains why most 'strategies' are just goals and slogans, and what a real strategy looks like.

## Key ideas

- **The kernel of good strategy:** a clear *diagnosis* of the challenge, a *guiding policy* for dealing with it, and *coherent actions* that carry it out.
- **Signs of bad strategy:** fluff and buzzwords, failure to face the real problem, mistaking goals for strategy, and vague or unrealistic objectives.
- **Leverage and focus.** Concentrate resources where they can have outsized effect.
- **Proximate objectives.** Set near-term goals that are achievable and move you forward.
- **Strategy as a hypothesis.** Treat it as a bet to be tested and refined, not a fixed plan.

## Takeaway

Start with an honest diagnosis. If you cannot name the core problem, you do not have a strategy.
