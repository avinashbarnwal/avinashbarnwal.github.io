*By D.A. Benton*

Debra Benton draws on interviews with top executives to describe the habits and mindsets that help people rise and lead effectively.

## Key ideas

- **Confidence and composure.** Leaders project calm and self-assurance, especially under pressure.
- **Relationships matter.** Senior leaders invest in people, networks and trust.
- **Simplify and decide.** Cut through complexity and make clear decisions.
- **Take risks and own outcomes.** Accept responsibility for both wins and failures.
- **Keep learning.** The best CEOs stay curious and keep improving.

## Takeaway

Thinking like a CEO is learnable: act with confidence, invest in people and take ownership.
