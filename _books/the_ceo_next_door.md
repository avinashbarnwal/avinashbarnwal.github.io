*By Elena L. Botelho*

Based on a large study of executive assessments, the authors show that successful CEOs are often not the polished, Ivy League stereotype, and identify the behaviours that really matter.

## Key ideas

- **Decide with speed and conviction.** Strong CEOs make decisions earlier and more often, even with incomplete information.
- **Engage for impact.** They understand stakeholders' needs and rally people around results.
- **Relentless reliability.** They deliver consistently, which builds trust.
- **Adapt boldly.** They let go of the past and embrace change.
- **Ordinary backgrounds, extraordinary results.** Many top leaders came from unexpected paths and had setbacks along the way.

## Takeaway

Leadership success comes from repeatable behaviours, not pedigree. Decide, engage, deliver and adapt.
