*By Virginia Evans*

A novel told entirely in letters, following Sybil Van Antwerp, a retired lawyer in her seventies who writes to family, old friends, authors she admires and strangers.

## Key ideas

- **Letters as a life.** Through decades of correspondence we see Sybil's sharp mind, her humour and the walls she has built around old grief.
- **Regret and repair.** The story slowly reveals the loss and estrangement that shaped her, and her hesitant steps toward forgiveness.
- **Connection in small acts.** A note to a neighbour or a reply to a stranger becomes a way of staying open to the world.
- **It is never too late.** Even late in life, people can change how they love and how they let others in.

## Takeaway

A quiet, moving reminder that honest words, written with care, can heal relationships we thought were finished.
