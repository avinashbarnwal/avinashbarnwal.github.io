*By April Jones Prince*

A short, illustrated biography of Samuel Clemens, the writer known as Mark Twain, from his boyhood on the Mississippi to worldwide fame.

## Key ideas

- **Life shapes stories.** His childhood in Hannibal, Missouri inspired Tom Sawyer and Huckleberry Finn.
- **Many careers.** He was a printer, riverboat pilot, miner and journalist before becoming a famous author.
- **Humour with purpose.** His wit often carried sharp criticism of injustice and hypocrisy.
- **Resilience.** He faced debts and personal loss and kept writing and lecturing.

## Takeaway

A quick, fun introduction to how Twain turned real-life adventures into lasting stories.
