*By Harvard Business Review*

A collection of key Harvard Business Review articles on starting, funding and scaling new ventures.

## Key ideas

- **Test before you build.** Validate customer demand with experiments before committing heavy resources.
- **Business model matters.** Great products fail without a viable way to create and capture value.
- **Funding with eyes open.** Understand what investors want and what you give up when you raise money.
- **Founder dilemmas.** Founders often face trade-offs between keeping control and building a bigger company.
- **Scaling is a new game.** What works at startup stage often breaks at scale; structure and leadership must evolve.

## Takeaway

Entrepreneurship is a disciplined process of learning, testing and adapting, not just a big idea.
