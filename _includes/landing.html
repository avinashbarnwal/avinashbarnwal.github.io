How my agents work

Plan. Act. Check. Remember.

  1. 01

    Plan

    Break a finance goal, like a 13-week cash forecast, into concrete steps.

  2. 02

    Act

    Call tools: ledgers, bank feeds, models and SQL, instead of guessing.

  3. 03

    Check

    Reconcile results against the books and flag exceptions for a human.

  4. 04

    Remember

    Keep context across runs so every cycle is sharper than the last.
